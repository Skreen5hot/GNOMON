## The Carrier Test

> The principle in ordinary language, anchored in one concrete scene. A principle that cannot
> walk a parent through a sleepover decision in plain words is not understood well enough to
> become architecture.

The test is a falsifier, not a style guide. Failure to write the carrier is evidence that the
formal essay has not been understood well enough to ground architecture — which is precisely
why `ARCH.md` (A-I.1) is blocked behind it in practice, even though nothing formally orders them.

The worked example of a passing carrier is `../judge-not/CARRIER.md` — *The Gavel and the Watch*
v1.0, which carries a five-strata jurisdiction map and a two-register principle through one
sleepover in plain words, with no jargon and no citations.

## What this carrier must carry

From `ESSAY.md`:

1. The fruits are **recognized, not measured.** They are lived qualities, not metrics.
2. **Formal systems illuminate; human persons judge.** This division of labor is the whole point.
3. **Absence is a question, not a verdict** (§6). This is the hardest one to say plainly without
   licensing exactly what it forbids.
4. **Legitimate absence exists** (§6.2) — and the prophetic case (§6.3): sometimes the person
   disturbing the peace is right, and the peace was the problem.
5. **Asymmetric application** (§7.3): fruits-talk applied downward against the less powerful is
   the characteristic abuse, and the essay says so.

## The difficulty, stated

Judge Not's carrier had a natural anchor scene: a decision, taken by one person, with a
deadline. The fruits have no equivalent. They describe a *state recognized over time by a
community* — which resists the single-scene form the Carrier Test requires.

Candidate anchors, none adjudicated:

| Candidate | Source | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| The hospital ethics committee | `ESSAY.md` §5.4 governance vignette | Institutional, not domestic; may not reach the reader the test is calibrated on |
| A person deciding whether to leave a church, a job, or a marriage that leaves them consistently anxious and contemptuous | new | Closest to the essay's diagnostic function; highest risk of licensing self-verdicts, which INV-03 and Judge Not §15 forbid |
| A parent watching an adult child's new relationship | new | Domestic and recognizably real; the "absence as question" move is naturally in frame |

**The trap to avoid.** A carrier that teaches "check whether the fruits are present in this
person" has taught the opposite of the essay: it converts a phenomenological indicator into a
test administered to another person, which is the algorithmic temptation (§1.3) and a strata-3/4
trespass in vernacular dress. The carrier's fidelity is gated at **CG**, and this is what the
gate is looking for.
